---
title: "Plans and payment"
description: "Free, Pro, Enterprise and Custom. Prices in Swiss francs, paid in Bitcoin, money-back guarantee."
---

| Plan | Checks per month | Per 30 days | One year in advance (launch price, first year) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 1,000 | CHF 0 | CHF 0 |
| Pro | 100,000 | CHF 7 | CHF 60 (CHF 5 a month) |
| Enterprise | 10,000,000 | CHF 150 | CHF 1,440 (CHF 120 a month) |
| Custom | as agreed | [contact form](https://gravitverifier.space/contact/) | |

- **How to pay:** get a free key, then on the [plans page](https://gravitverifier.space/docs/#buy) enter it, choose the plan and the period, and pay with Bitcoin through BTCPay Server. The payment goes straight to the Foundation's own wallet.
- **After payment:** a confirmation arrives from `billing@gravitverifier.space`; your key stays the same.
- **No VAT** is charged: the Gravit Open Network Foundation, a Swiss association, is not VAT registered.
- **Money-back guarantee:** write to us through the [contact form](https://gravitverifier.space/contact/) within 30 days of payment and we refund the full amount in Bitcoin. No questions asked.

The plans are also available as data: `GET https://gravitverifier.space/api/v1/plans`.
