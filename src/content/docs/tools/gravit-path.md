---
title: "Gravit Path"
description: "A verified route from who you are to what you are legally allowed to do."
---

Gravit Path is an assistant for people who want to start a business, a club or a non-profit. It says first where you cannot go, then shows two to four lawful paths, and prepares the documents. It answers only from published rules, each with a dated official source.

- Website and all rules: [gravitpath.world](https://gravitpath.world)
- Principles and rule packs: [gravit-path](https://github.com/gravit-open-network-foundation/gravit-path)

Status: rule packs for the United Kingdom and Switzerland; the assistant is in development.
